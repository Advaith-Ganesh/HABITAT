# Hackathon submission · 150 words

HABITAT AI helps housing caseworkers inspect fictional applications that may need additional human review. It never allocates homes, determines statutory eligibility or rejects applicants. The runnable sandbox combines React, FastAPI, SQLite and one trained Random Forest with genuine SHAP explanations. A generator publishes 2,000 synthetic records, split into 1,400 training, 300 validation and 300 test records with related households isolated. Test accuracy is 77.7%, recall 72.9% and F1 67.6%. A transparent checklist achieves higher recall, making the workload trade-off visible. Fairlearn exposes group error differences instead of claiming universal fairness. Two hundred separate counterfactual pairs test invariance to an excluded audit attribute. Mandatory override reasons and append-only events preserve human oversight. The England reference distinguishes statutory guidance from fictional intake assumptions. Proposed subscription tiers and an assumption-driven calculator support pilot discussions. Real-world validation, production security and verified local policy packs remain prerequisites before any operational use with real housing applicants.
